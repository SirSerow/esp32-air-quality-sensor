# Integrated air-quality PCB requirements

Revision: 0.4, 2026-09-11. Status: PRODUCT SCOPE AND OPERATING PROFILE AGREED; engineering qualification and remaining acceptance limits pending.

This document defines the intended KiCad 10 implementation and its acceptance evidence. It does not declare the existing board validated. Confirmed product choices and proposed engineering limits are distinguished below. Component qualification, power budgeting and mechanical feasibility studies may proceed. Detailed schematic/layout implementation requires resolution of dependent constraints. No manufacturing release shall contain unresolved mandatory requirements.

## 1. Verified starting point

- README.md describes ESP32-C6-DevKitC-1, AHT20, ENS160, SSD1306 128x64 I2C OLED, microSD logging, NVS fallback, Wi-Fi station/SoftAP and web/API access.
- src/app_config.h: I2C SDA GPIO6, SCL GPIO7, 100 kHz; OLED address 0x3C; display button GPIO5; SD MISO21/MOSI22/CLK19/CS20; sampling every 60 seconds; NVS capacity 180 records.
- platformio.ini configures 2 MB flash and a single-application partition table. This is a software configuration, not proof of the fitted flash capacity.
- Existing sensor-pcb files were generated by KiCad 9 and use a DevKit footprint plus socket/module interfaces. They are a reference, not an approved integrated circuit.
- Current src/sensor_loop.c reads AHT temperature/humidity but contains no ENS160 compensation write. New measurement requirements must cover hardware and firmware together.

## 2. Requirement rules

Each final requirement shall contain an ID, one unambiguous SHALL statement, its numerical limits where relevant, verification method, and evidence reference. Verification status shall be NOT RUN, PASS, FAIL, or justified NOT APPLICABLE. Calculated, simulated, and measured evidence shall remain distinguishable. Unavailable models or equipment shall be recorded; they shall not produce a PASS.

Component operating limits shall come from the exact selected manufacturer's datasheet revision. Absolute maximum ratings shall not be used as normal operating limits. Conflicting constraints shall be resolved before dependent design work proceeds. Requirement changes after baseline shall record the decision and repeat affected checks.

## 5. Completion gates

1. Requirements baseline: D01-D08 resolved; measurable limits and interfaces agreed; no architecture-critical TBDs.
2. Schematic review: exact parts, power calculations, pin mapping, connectivity assertions, ERC and firmware implications checked.
3. Layout review: mechanics, thermal/airflow, RF, assembly access, configured fabrication rules and DRC checked.
4. Fabrication package complete: all release outputs regenerated from the same reviewed revision, visually inspected and documented. This is design completion, not proof of working hardware.
5. Prototype validated: physical bring-up, measurement, reliability and enclosure tests passed. Record failures and required board revisions. A first-pass success cannot be guaranteed by requirements or CAD checks alone.

## 7. Confirmed scope and proposed numerical baseline

User-confirmed scope takes precedence over generic alternatives in section 3: SHT40/SGP40 replace AHT20/ENS160, microSD is removed, battery operation and OTA are mandatory, and the existing DevKit is excluded. References to OLED in generic tests mean the final selected display. Exact orderable components remain subject to qualification, not a released BOM.

| ID | Status | Requirement / proposed acceptance criterion |
|---|---|---|
| INT-01 | Confirmed | One custom factory-assembled PCB with a compact factory-mountable radio module permitted; no plug-in development board or hand-wired sensor breakout boards. |
| SUP-01 | Confirmed, evidence rule proposed | Every populated PCB BOM line must have an exact assembler inventory ID, verified stock sufficient for the order plus assembler attrition, supported assembly process and timestamped evidence before design freeze and release. Display and Wi-Fi may sleep otherwise. Local web access is available while Wi-Fi is awake. Optimize beyond 48 hours where feasible without compromising sensing. |
| BAT-02 | Continuous sensing confirmed; implementation proposed | Sensing shall continue on battery, USB and while charging. Proposed protected rechargeable single-cell Li-ion/LiPo battery, temperature-aware charging, defined charge-current limit and load-sharing/power-path management. Verify USB only, battery only, both and transitions; no reset or corruption during normal transitions. Battery chemistry/capacity remain open. |
| UI-01 | Confirmed preference | Small colour display preferred, economical and factory mountable. Proposed class: 0.96-1.14 inch SPI TFT with controllable backlight. Validate readable numeric views before committing panel size. |
| UI-02 | Confirmed; performance limits pending | Two controllable indicator LEDs, miniature buzzer, and digital five-way navigation switch (up/down/left/right/press). Freeze sound level/distance, mute behavior and brightness limits. |
| LOG-01 | 28-day retention confirmed; encoding/capacity proposed | Dedicated nonvolatile memory IC; retain at least 28 days of 60-second records without network access. Proposed maximum record size 64 bytes including timestamp, sequence, validity and CRC: 40,320 records = 2,580,480 bytes. Proposed 8 MiB NOR allows metadata, erase management and reserve; actual format/partition must prove capacity. |
| LOG-02 | Proposed | Power interruption may lose at most the currently uncommitted 60-second sample; previously committed records remain recoverable. Use recoverable append records, CRC, sequence numbers and wear management. Test interruptions during programming, erase and metadata updates. |
| LOG-03 | Proposed | Reconnection uploads backlog without silent duplicates or gaps using stable record IDs/acknowledgments. Retain data until acknowledged within capacity; explicitly signal overflow if the network outage exceeds capacity. |
| RTC-01 | 28-day backup confirmed; accuracy proposed | Preserve time over USB removal and MCU resets without network access, including 28 days after main battery disconnection/depletion. Proposed accuracy: within 60 seconds over 28 days at 10-35 C. Backup component and worst-case leakage/accuracy calculations required; main-battery-only operation does not satisfy complete power-loss retention. |
| OTA-01 | Confirmed scope; checks proposed | Wireless updates mandatory. Provide two application slots and rollback; reject invalid/unauthenticated images, recover from interrupted download or first boot, preserve logs/settings. Prove partition capacity using compiled firmware plus specified growth margin before freezing MCU flash size. |
| SIZE-01 | Confirmed PCB envelope; enclosure thickness goal | PCB outline shall fit within 54 x 43 mm (2,322 mm2). Target external enclosure thickness below 10 mm, including controls/protrusions, subject to feasibility. Include wall thickness, display, PCB, battery maximum dimensions/expansion allowance, tolerances and assembly clearances. Enclosure XY dimensions remain to derive. If sub-10 mm is infeasible, present the minimum justified thickness before changing the target. |
| ASM-01 | Confirmed | Target three factory-soldered PCB assemblies. User may insert display and battery connectors and close the enclosure; no user soldering shall be required. Factory assembly support for connectors, sensors and all soldered parts must be verified. Provide illustrated final assembly and programming instructions. |
| COST-01 | Confirmed | Total cost shall be below USD 200 for three PCB assemblies including populated components, displays, fabrication, setup/stencil, assembly, shipping and applicable taxes. Batteries, enclosures and reference instruments are excluded. Obtain a complete quote before release. Battery/enclosure costs shall still be reported separately; their exclusion from budget does not remove their design/fit requirements. Current feasibility is NOT VERIFIED. |
| ENV-01 | Temperature/use confirmed; RH endpoints pending | Support normal sensing and operation indoors at 10-35 C in non-condensing conditions. Select components and charging behavior compatible with this environment. Define quantitative RH endpoints for measurement qualification before sensor acceptance testing. Verify thermal effects while charging and at maximum normal load in the enclosure. |
| MEAS-01 | Sensor choice confirmed | Revision 1 shall measure temperature/RH using SHT40 and report SGP40 VOC Index with validity status. Store sensor/algorithm identity and schema version. Do not relabel VOC Index as TVOC ppb or CO2 ppm, or silently join it to old ENS160 metrics. Direct CO2 is not part of the current working scope. |
| VALID-02 | Proposed | Run all three prototypes for 72 hours with no unexplained reset, missing scheduled committed samples or unrecoverable storage failure. Include separate power/OTA interruption and declared battery-runtime tests; this test does not establish production reliability. |

The 28-day retention targets, PCB dimensions, battery usage profile, budget exclusions and indoor temperature range are accepted. Record format, battery chemistry, clock accuracy, detailed bench conditions and test duration remain engineering proposals. Sampling cadence for sensor algorithms may be faster than the stored one-minute record rate; reducing logging frequency does not by itself reduce sensor heater power.

Battery runtime shall be calculated in energy units using the chosen cell's usable capacity, discharge cutoff, conversion efficiency, quiescent current and measured load profile. Nominal battery mAh divided by a sensor's current at a different rail voltage is not a valid whole-device runtime prediction. Include radio reconnect failures, backlight use and sensor conditioning. Charge heat must be included in temperature/RH validation.

## 9. Practical measurement references

- Temperature/RH: a separate SHT45 evaluation setup is a practical comparison instrument. It must be thermally separated from its host electronics and used under specified conditions. A factory sensor specification is not a traceable calibration certificate for the finished setup.
- Direct CO2: Sensirion SEK-SCD41 is a practical independent CO2 comparison setup (https://sensirion.com/products/catalog/SEK-SCD41). It cannot validate ENS160 eCO2 as though both measured the same quantity.
- VOC: a second MOX sensor can compare event response/repeatability but does not validate absolute TVOC concentration. Formal accuracy acceptance needs a defined analyte/gas mixture, reference method and conditions; otherwise specify trend/repeatability tests, not unsupported ppb accuracy.
- Before procurement, set reference-instrument budget and decide whether practical comparisons suffice for revision 1. No reference equipment was purchased.
